# Intelligence

Most software matches text. We're interested in software that understands it.

Our work on intelligence asks how a system can take in a question, the context around it and the material it has access to, and work out what is being asked before it answers.

## What we're exploring

- **Context, not keywords.** Understanding the intent behind a question, even when it's phrased loosely.
- **Reasoning over knowledge.** Connecting pieces of information that live in different places.
- **Knowing the limits.** Being clear about what a system knows, and what it doesn't.
- **Natural interaction.** Responding fast enough, and naturally enough, that it feels like talking to a person.

## Where it shows up

- [Ucelot](/products/ucelot) applies it to codebases and documents.
- The [Interactive Smart Conversation Engine](/products/conversation-engine) applies it to real-time conversation.
